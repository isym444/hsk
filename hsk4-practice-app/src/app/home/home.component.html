The questions come from the official HSK Level 4 sample papers compiled by Hanban / the Confucius Institute Headquarters. The listening sections play the official HSK 4 recordings (H41001–H41003): each question's own clip, cut from the real listening test so the voice and pace match the exam — press Play to listen. The second writing task (write a sentence from a picture) originally came with an illustration, which is omitted here: only the prompt word is kept, and you can self-assess against the model answer once you have written your sentence.
